# Code of conduct

Midad follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive
criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Calligraphy is a tradition with many schools and masters. Disagreements about
style are welcome; contempt for another school is not.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers (address listed in
the repository settings). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant apply.
